About the Central Library

Established in 1984, the VIT Central Library is a cornerstone of academic life. We support the research and learning needs of all Engineering and Management programs, offering both digital and physical resources to foster innovation and knowledge. Our mission is to provide world-class information services to the VIT community.

Overview and Collection

The Bajrangdas Lohiya Central Library is the main knowledge hub of VIT Pune. Established in 1984, its primary goal is to support the institute's educational and research activities by providing comprehensive access to information. The library has a substantial physical and digital collection, which includes over 82,400 items such as textbooks, reference books, handbooks, and e-books. It also subscribes to 97 national and international journals and periodicals, supplemented by over 1,500 CDs and more than 500 B.I. (Bureau of Indian Standards) standards.

Digital Resources and Services

Beyond its print collection, the library provides significant digital resources. It offers access to major academic consortia and databases, including J-Gate, Shodhganga, E-ShodhSindhu, and the National Digital Library of India (NDLI). The library is a member of DELNET (Developing Library Network), which facilitates resource sharing. Key services for students and faculty include a plagiarism checking facility using iThenticate software and an Inter-Library Loan (ILL) service in collaboration with VIIT and VU.

Facilities and Infrastructure

The library is organized into several key sections to cater to different academic needs. These include a Reference Section for in-depth study, a Journal Section for current periodicals, a general Stack-Room for the main book collection, and a Reading Hall for quiet study. The library also provides reprographic (photocopying) facilities for students. The entire collection is managed using the SLIM 21 library management software, ensuring efficient operation.

🏛️ Key Sections

  • Reference Section: For in-depth study and research
  • Journal Section: Current periodicals and magazines
  • Stack-Room: Main book collection area
  • Reading Hall: Quiet study spaces
  • Digital Section: Computer access for research
  • Reprographic Services: Photocopying facilities

Access and Operation

Students can search the library's entire catalog and check the availability of books using the Online Public Access Catalogue (WEB OPAC). The circulation (issue and return) of books is streamlined through a barcode-based system. While the specific number of books a student can borrow and the exact fine policy are typically detailed in the student handbook or on the internal college portal, the general rules require users to maintain silence and carry their official institute ID card for entry.
