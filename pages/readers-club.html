Our Mission & Vision

In the fast-paced world of engineering and technology, the VIT Readers Club serves as a sanctuary for the mind. We believe that reading is not merely a pastime but a crucial tool for developing empathy, critical thinking, and creativity. Our mission is to foster a vibrant literary culture on campus where students from diverse technical backgrounds can come together to explore worlds beyond equations and code.

Founded in 2018 by a small group of avid readers, the club has grown into a bustling community. We aim to bridge the gap between the arts and sciences, proving that a good engineer is also a well-read individual. Whether it’s dissecting the complex narratives of Dostoevsky or debating the ethical implications of AI in Isaac Asimov’s works, we provide the platform for intellectual exchange.

📝 Monthly Editorial: "The Paper Resilience"

By Aarav P., Club Secretary (TY CS)

"In an era dominated by scrolling feeds and 15-second videos, one might ask: is the physical book dying? The answer, evidenced by the attendance at our last book fair, is a resounding no. There is a tactile resilience to paper that screens cannot replicate. The smell of old glue, the texture of a page turn, the spatial memory of knowing exactly where on the page a favorite quote resides—these are experiences that ground us."

"This month, we are focusing on 'Slow Reading.' We encourage our members to put down their phones, pick up a classic, and read not for completion, but for immersion. Let us reclaim our attention spans, one chapter at a time."
