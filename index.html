Book Rental Guidelines

  1. Eligibility: Valid VIT ID card must be presented for all transactions.
  2. Max Books: FY Students can borrow a maximum of 2 books at a time.
  3. Loan Period: The loan period is 10 days.
  4. Renewal: Books may be renewed once, provided there are no pending reservations.
  5. Late Fees: A fine of ₹1 per day will be levied on books returned after the due date.

Computer Lab Usage Conditions

The library provides dedicated PCs for academic use. Please adhere to the following:

  • PCs are strictly for educational and research purposes.
  • Downloading or installing unauthorized software is prohibited.
  • Users must log out after their session.
  • Silence must be maintained in the PC usage area.
  • Personal data (USB drives) must be scanned for viruses before use.
